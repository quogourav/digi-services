FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A starter site usually takes 1–2 weeks and a 5-page business site 2–4 weeks, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback.

Do I own my website and accounts?

Yes. Your domain, hosting and analytics accounts are set up in your name, and we hand over full access at launch.

How soon will SEO show results?

SEO is a steady build rather than an overnight switch. Local improvements can appear within a few weeks; competitive searches typically take a few months. We'll never promise a guaranteed ranking.

Are the tool recommendations paid placements?

We use these tools ourselves. Some links are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you sign up, at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we'd use on our own projects.

How does the partner programme pay out?

Once a referred client's project is paid for, your commission is calculated and paid out. Details and rates are shared when your application is approved.
